# Product inquiries use Messenger

The site introduces Mom and showcases products. Product inquiries use each
business's configured Messenger destination; ordering and payment happen off-site.
This keeps the website within the agreed scope without checkout, payment handling,
or a contact form. Navigation and photo controls have their own actions.

Missing or invalid Messenger destinations lead to a contact-unavailable notice.
Product links carry a reference identifier, but the site does not promise prefilled
messages, human-visible product context, or confirmation that a message was sent.
There is no inquiry analytics or conversion tracking.

The earlier rationale assumed made-to-order, personalised products and established
Messenger usage. Those business facts are unverified and were withdrawn as design
assumptions on 11 October 2026. The confirmed decision is a showcase with Messenger inquiries. On 23 October 2026,
the owner confirmed handmade personalized balloon art and Unicity product resale.
This confirms the categories, not the earlier personalisation or production claims
about Unicity products. Contact destinations and ordering terms remain pending.
