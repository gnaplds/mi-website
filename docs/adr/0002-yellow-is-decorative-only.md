# Yellow leads the surfaces; dark text carries contrast

Mom prefers yellow as the main colour, with green and brown and a light, natural
feel. The accepted design uses yellow headers, introductions, and contact footers,
with light-yellow content backgrounds and dark text. Green carries primary buttons;
green and brown distinguish the businesses' product headings.

Use dark ink on yellow and white text on green. Yellow against white does not
provide sufficient text contrast; the presence of yellow in the palette is not a
reason to use it for small text on white.

The decorative-only emphasis was superseded by the user's preference on
11 October 2026. This file retains its original name to preserve existing links;
its current decision is the surface treatment above. The earlier cream-first design
is historical, not a rebuild requirement.

On 23 October 2026, the owner approved a softer yellow and a warm handmade
boutique direction. Yellow remains the main surface colour; the contrast rules
and supporting roles of green and brown are unchanged. Exact tokens live in
`PLAN.md` and `assets/css/styles.css`.
