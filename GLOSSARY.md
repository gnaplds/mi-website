# Winnie Works

A personal showcase for Winnie and her two businesses. Visitors learn about her,
browse products, and contact the relevant business; transactions happen off-site.

## Language

**Mom**:
Winnie, the person featured on the site and the owner of both businesses. She supplies
photos and descriptions; the site owner maintains the website.

**Business**:
One of Winnie’s two ventures: Personalized Balloon Art (Business A) or Unicity
Products (Business B).
_Avoid_: store, shop (these suggest online transactions)

**Sample balloon design**:
An illustrative idea for personalized balloon art, organised around an occasion.
It is not a confirmed catalogue entry or a promise of availability.

**Product**:
Something a business showcases and a visitor can inquire about. It is not a
purchasable unit on this website.
_Avoid_: SKU, listing

**Variant**:
An informational description of available product options. Visitors discuss their
preference with the business rather than selecting it on the website.
_Avoid_: selection (implies an interactive control)

**Inquiry**:
A visitor's attempt to contact a business about its products through Messenger.
Opening a link does not establish that a message was sent or received.

**Inquiry channel**:
The Messenger destination assigned to a business. Each business has its own
configured destination for inquiries.

**Grid layout**:
A product presentation using equal-width cards arranged in rows. It is the chosen
presentation for Business A.

**Showcase layout**:
A product presentation using spacious rows with alternating image and text positions.
It is the chosen presentation for Business B.

**Theme**:
A business's supporting accent colour: green for A and brown for B. Yellow remains
the main colour throughout the site.
_Avoid_: palette (the palette is shared)

**Palette**:
The shared set of yellow, light yellow, green, brown, dark ink, and white colours.

**Placeholder**:
Temporary text or imagery awaiting final content from Winnie. English sample
descriptions and photo stand-ins remain placeholders even when they are readable.

**Deployed preview**:
The publicly hosted website while placeholder content remains. Deployment alone
does not mean the business content is ready for launch.
